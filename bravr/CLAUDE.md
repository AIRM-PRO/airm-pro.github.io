# Orbital Brawl (airm-pro.github.io/bravr)

VR brawler for Meta Quest 2, played in the Quest Browser via WebXR. The player wears wrist weights. The original hand-speed limit (fast swings dealt no damage) was removed on request in Oct 2026: harder punches now shove enemies further. The only safety nudge left is a tutorial line about not snapping the elbow straight.

## Game design

- The player stands on a deck in a 2×2 m doorway of a steel ship hull (`base`, `placeBase()` on every `setAnchor`; hull face at `WALL_Z` = −0.8 m behind the anchor) floating above a planet; the old round platform is hidden. Armored soldiers fly in from 8–11 m (superman pose, weaving, boot thruster sparks, cruising up to 3× `SPEED` when far and braking on arrival).
- **Rings and constant pressure** (since v0.10): melee enemies spawn towards the *waiting* line (`RING.wait`, 1.3 m, ±50°), bombers onto the *outer* line (2.4 m, ±45°). `charges()` sends a rested (`e.rest`, 0.3–0.8 s) waiting enemy, or one still flying in from deep space (`e.fresh`, it turns without stopping), to a free spot on the *inner* ring (0.65 m, ±45°) when fewer than `strikeCap` = `min(1 + round/2, 3)` are in, 0.2–0.5 s apart. It winds up and punches once; then `reengage()` lets it drift back 0.5–0.75 m (`e.backTo`) and curve straight back in to the same spot, unless a rested enemy is waiting, in which case `toWait()` sends it to the waiting line and the other one gets the spot. Shoves and throws end in `toWait` too (the tumble's velocity carries over), with no breather and `e.fresh` set, so on the way back it can take a free strike spot without stopping on the waiting line; it only parks there when all strike spots are taken. A spot is free when the arc distance to every other enemy on that ring clears both torsos (`freeAngle`, torso radius `bodyR = 0.2 · scale · bulk`).
- **Movement (steering):** in `approach` an enemy has a velocity `e.sv` that `steer()` turns towards the goal with limited acceleration (`ACCEL` 2.4 m/s² at slow tempo, scaled with `SPEED`) and brakes in time to arrive (max speed `SPEED` × `speedMul`, up to ×3 when more than 2 m out). No dashes, no instant stops: `updateEnemies` measures every enemy's real velocity (`e.vel`) and `approach` starts from it, so leaving a hover, a recoil or a shove continues in one curve. The pose follows the speed (`e.fly`, `e.retreat` = moving away). Hits (`kv`), `separate()` pushes and blasts still jolt them, which is intended.
- **At most `FAR_MAX` = 2 wait out of reach** (hovering on the waiting or outer line, bombers included). `meleeFits` only lets a melee enemy spawn if the ones the outer ring can't hold (assumed the biggest) fit on the strike arc side by side (`strikeFits`, worst order) and number ≤ `strikeCap`; a brute that doesn't fit becomes a soldier or grunt. With `strikeCap` > 1 attackers pack against a neighbour or the arc's end (`freeAngle(…, pack)`); if a rested enemy still doesn't fit, `compact()` slides the attackers out to the ends (not mid-swing or held). More than 2 waiting: the breather is skipped. Measured with `__obSim`: over 2 under 0.5% of the time, in blips ≤ 0.1 s. `separate()` also pushes overlapping torsos apart every frame and keeps them ≥ 0.36 m from the player. Nobody stands still: they strafe, bob and rock (`hold`).
- **Enemy attack:** `windup` (`CHARGE[type]`: grunt 0.8, soldier 0.7, brute 1.0 s, fixed so the rhythm can be learned; the robot leans back and turns away, the punching fist (`arms[1]`) rises beside its head and the other arm comes up as a guard: pose `WINDUP`, found by a grid search for those fist positions; it trembles in the last quarter, visor heats) → `swing` (`SWING` 0.36 s: `aimArm` gives the shoulder angles that point the arm from its shoulder at your face, so the fist really travels at you; the body throws its weight forward and lunges 0.22 m; lands at `IMPACT` = 55%, 0.2 s after the swing starts; holds at full reach until `SWING_HOLD` 80%; a glowing trail follows the fist) → `recover` (`RECOVER` 0.35 s) → `reengage`. **Telegraph** (`telegraph(e)`, every frame): a ring round the fist, facing you and drawn over everything (`depthTest:false`), shrinks from 22 cm onto a fixed 7 cm inner ring and closes exactly at impact (yellow → red, white in the last 0.15 s); a sawtooth whine panned to the attacker rises 140 → 790 Hz (`whine`); `quiet(e)` hides and stops both (any other state, death, breaks). `SFX.enemySwing` cracks as the swing starts. **Landing on you** (`hitPlayer`): the attacker freezes at full reach for 0.12 s (`e.stop`), `hurtPlayer(n, from)` plays `SFX.hurt` from its side and lights the red arc on that edge of your view (`hurtArcs`, two partial spheres kept level with your gaze; a weaker full-view flash on top). The swing is **blocked** if either of your hands is within 0.13 m of its fist before impact, and **dodged** if your head has moved more than 0.22 m since the swing started. Otherwise you take 1 HP (brute: 2). A landed hit is still stored as `leaks` (history compat) and shown as "Oberwałeś".
- **Player HP:** `PLAYER_HP` = 10 on the left wrist watch; **mana** (`MANA` 10, `MANA_REGEN` 0.5/s during waves) on the right one (`watches`, `drawWatches`). At 0 HP: knockout (`G.kos`), enemies blown back, HP refills, and the most recent upgrade is lost. HP and mana refill at every rest.
- **Shield, one-hit kills and stun:** `TYPES` give shields grunt 0, soldier 2, brute 4, and 1 HP each. `hit(e, kind, h, at, dir)` drains the shield first (`DRAIN`); once it is down any hit kills grunts and soldiers through `crush()` (style `decap` for a neck chop or a punch to the head, otherwise `shatter` (the robot falls apart into head, arms, chest and hips) or `punch` (limbs torn); blasts and bumps always shatter). Brutes have `stuns:true`: their first hit after the shield is stunned instead. A punch on a shield `shove`s the enemy (`SHOVE` 5.2 m/s, about 2.2 m) into state `shoved`; it tumbles, then goes back to `approach` and flies to its front slot. The shield regenerates after 5 s without a hit. `stun()` → state `stunned` for `STUN` 4 s, then any hit kills (`finish`). Chop to the neck and the rip are **fatalities** (`G.fatal`, +3 mana). An unfinished stun recovers with no shield (the next hit stuns again). Status bars over the head and a field bubble come from `e.showStatus()`.
- **Grenades:** **bombers** throw them: from wave 1 one enemy (two from wave 5, `bomberCap`) spawns on the outer ring with `e.bomber` and is never promoted; they count toward the 2 out-of-reach spots (`meleeFits`). `wantSpawn` sends a melee enemy first and a bomber only once a melee enemy is in play, so a wave opens with someone to fight (before v0.10 the bomber always came first and the first punch landed ~9 s in; now ~4.5 s). In `hover` it cocks an arm and `throwBomb`s every `rnd(9, 13) − 0.3·round` s (max 2 live): a high lob (0.9–1.8 s by distance) that stops about 0.42 m in front of your chest and drifts closer until `BOMB.fuse` 3.2 s. They glow, trail sparks, and blink and beep faster near the end. Catch: a loose hand closing into a fist (`u.grabEv`) within 0.15 m (a grenade in reach wins over grabbing an enemy); throw: release the grip (hand velocity × 1.3). A thrown grenade sticks to the enemy it hits, shoves it and blows 0.35 s later. A fist moving into one sets it off. `explode` hurts the player (1 HP within `BOMB.radius` 0.7 m of the chest) and `hit`s enemies with `blast`.
- **Bilard** (always on since v0.7): a `shoved` enemy faster than 2 m/s `hit`s whoever it touches with `bump`; a `thrown` one also takes a `bump` itself on the first collision, on the hull, or when it slows down without hitting anything.
- **Pause:** A/X or B/Y (`buttons[4]`/`[5]`, edge-detected in `updateHands`) calls `togglePause()`: `loop` only updates head and hands and renders, with the `tut` panel (moves `TUT` + owned upgrades' `how`) in front of you.
- **Upgrade tree:** `UPGRADES[id] = {branch, name, max, req, desc[per level], how}`; branches `BRANCHES` (PIĘŚĆ, DŁOŃ, OCZY, CZAS, CIAŁO). `G.ups` is the pick order, `lvl(id)` counts picks, `has(id)` = lvl > 0; a KO pops the last pick. `offerCards` skips maxed upgrades and ones whose `req` isn't owned. `maxHp()` (+2 per `armor`), `maxMana()` (+3 per `tank`), `manaRegen()` (+30% per `tank`). In the break `showStats()` draws the `stats` character sheet (stats + tree with level pips) left of the cards.
- **Szczęka (jaw):** separate array `jaws` (`makeJaw`, `spawnJaws` from wave 2, `jawCap` 1, 2 from wave 4; `updateJaws`). States approach → stage (2.3 m) → come → front (chomp: open 0.7 s, shut 0.6 s, ×3) → lunge (bites the face unless dodged; a hand in the way is bitten instead) → back to stage. `JAW_HP` 3 hits: a fist/blade moving mostly vertically (> 1 m/s) on top/bottom while `open` < 0.35 (`smackJaw`, tumbles up/down and returns), the free hand hitting it while it bites, a thrown wreck, the laser. Any other fist/blade contact = `biteHand`: `u.bitten`, the hand is forced `loose` (unless `gloves`), gnaws 1 HP every 2.5 s, lets go after 6 s. Two hands closing (`grabEv`) on its sides = `held` (one hand alone: it bites after 0.8 s); release in a swing throws it (Bilard `bump`); with `vise` both hands down at > 2 m/s = `crushed`; with `gloves` the other fist closing inside a biting jaw = `pry`, pull 0.22 m apart vertically = `split` (fatality, +3 mana). While it bites, `jawTag` floats a label (what to do, hits, seconds left) and the glove throbs red. `killJaw` chars it (`charJaw`, `JAW_DEAD`): `burst` and `crushed` leave one wreck (slack or flattened, some teeth knocked out); only `split` makes two halves.
- **Wrecks:** `kill()` hands the main body pieces to `wreck(obj, vel, spin, r)` instead of `addDebris`: the fling eases into a 0.18 m/s drift away, they push enemies/jaws aside, can be grabbed (`grabEv`) and thrown (`bump` on enemies, `smackJaw` on jaws), and shrink away after 40 s or 10 m (max 10, `dropWreck`). Cleared at rests.
- **Powers** (`updatePowers`, work steps only): **laser** — a loose hand at the side of the head (< 0.2 m, beside not in front) shows the `crosshair` along `head.dir`; switching it to blade (`u.was === 'loose'`) fires for `LASER[lvl]` s (0.3/1/2), 2 mana, `hit(e,'laser')` every 0.3 s along the gaze ray, red `blind` overlay while firing. **Bóg prędkości** — both hands > 0.2 m behind the head plane for 0.15 s, 3 mana: world `gdt` × `SLOW[lvl].k` for `SLOW[lvl].dur` (`slowK()`), blue `chrono` overlay, 3 s cooldown.
- **Upgrades** (`UPGRADES`, `G.ups`): each rest offers 3 cards (`offerCards`) and you punch one. `haishu` (a blade-hand backhand bats a grenade back, 1 mana), `squat` (dodging with the head ≥ 25 cm lower than at swing start gives +2 mana), `charge` (a fist still for 1 s glows; the next punch is `charged`: drain 3, double shove), `thunder` (needs `charge`; fists slammed together in front of the chest at > 1.2 m/s closing speed, 4 mana: shields to 0 within 1 m, shove), `vise`, `gloves` (needs `vise`), `laser` ×3, `speed` ×3, `armor` ×3, `tank` ×3 (see above).
- Session = 6 s countdown, then N waves with a break after each, then the **secret level** (below), then an end screen. A **break has no timer** (`dur: Infinity`): the player punches an upgrade card and then the **DALEJ** button (`nextBtn`, `advance()`). `cfg.rest` is only the suggested length shown on the HUD. Pull a trigger to exit at the end.
- **Sound:** every sound goes through `bus` (`makeBus`: gain → compressor → limiter). `sample(group, vol, rate, pan)` plays a random take from `SAMPLES` (rate < 1 = lower and heavier); `thump(f0, f1, len, vol, drive)` is a falling sine through a tanh `WaveShaper` so its overtones carry the low end on the Quest's small speakers; `crack` is a 35 ms high-passed noise click. `SFX.punch(p, pan)` (the metal slam, plus a quiet `crack` and `thump`), `shieldHit`, `crunch(p, pan, big)` (kills), `superHit` (uppercut, hammer) and `block` layer these; `SFX.death(pan, rate)` plays the death sound 60 ms after the impact on every robot kill (`kill`, rate grunt 1.05 / soldier 0.97 / brute 0.85) and jaw kill (1.15); `p` is `power(h)` (hand speed 0.5–3.5 m/s → 0.15–1) and `pan` is `panOf(at)`. Landed hits also buzz the controller harder with `p` and freeze the enemy for 40–90 ms (**hit-stop**, `e.stop`, skipped in `updateEnemies`). **Voice:** an enemy coming within 1.1 m (`e.near`, reset past 1.6 m; not shoved, held or stunned) barks `SFX.voice` (a random line from `voiceBag`, no repeats until all 40 have played; brute rate 0.88), at most one line every 2.5–4 s (`G.voiceT`). **Heartbeat:** `heartbeat(k, tempo)` plays during waves only (not in breaks, not paused; `heartbeatOff` fades it out and stops the scheduled beats). It schedules the file's 15 beats one by one (`HEART_BEAT` 0.476 s slices with 5 ms fades, 0.3 s ahead), `HEART_BEAT / tempo` apart, so the tempo changes without changing the pitch: `heartTempo(round)` = 0.5 × 1.1^(round − 1) (wave 1 half speed, wave 6 ≈ 0.8). Routed into the limiter after the compressor (`bus.lim`) so it doesn't pump the hits. Tension `k` = 0.7 × wave progress + 0.3 × wave number: gain 0.2 → 0.38 (RMS about −24 → −19 dB; a hard punch is about −12), low-pass 0.9 → 3.9 kHz. **The Pope's voice** (`john-paul-audio/`, see `jp-sounds.md`): `SFX.pope(group, pan, cut)` plays a random clip from `popeHit` on every hit he takes and from `popeHurt` (with `cut`) when his punch lands on you; `POPE_ODDS` weights the pick per group in list order (`popeHurt` [3, 1]: „Zrozumiano” on 3 hits in 4). One line at a time (`popeLine`): a line asked for while he's still talking is dropped, unless `cut`, which fades the old one out. The intro (`popeStart`, 20 s) is separate (`popeIntro`) and ducks to 30% under his lines. Clips play at `POPE_VOICE` 0.8 × `sampleGain` (each levelled to −18 dB RMS over its louder half of 50 ms windows, gains measured 0.5–1.4); `SFX.popeHush()` stops him when the fight ends. He doesn't use the robots' voice lines. `__obDebug().popeVoice()` shows what's playing and each clip's length and gain; its `pick(group, n)` counts n picks (checks the odds). `__obSfx(play, raw)` renders a sound offline and measures it (old synth punch RMS −26 dB, the metal-slam punch −12.5 dB).
- **Secret level (the Pope):** the last step of every game (`buildPlan`: a `work` step with `boss:true`, `dur:Infinity`, after the break that follows the last wave), or on its own from **Wejdź do VR: sekretny poziom** (in mouse mode **Dev: sekretny poziom**, shown with the dev button). Each start button sets `mode` through `start(m, f)`: `'game'` or `'pope'`. The landing page links `bravr/?sekret`, which only scrolls to that button. No waves, jaws or other spawns. His intro line (`SFX.popeStart`) opens it: on its own it starts with the 6 s countdown and he arrives as the countdown ends; after a full game it starts with the fight and he arrives `POPE_CFG.delay` 2.5 s into it (`G.popeT`; the HUD says PAPIEŻ NADCHODZI until then). If the clip hasn't loaded yet, it plays the moment it arrives (`popeIntroWait`). `spawnPope()` brings him down from 3 m above, 5 m ahead, and he then fights like any melee enemy (charges in, winds up, punches for 2 HP like a brute, `CHARGE.pope` 0.9 s), only heavier (`TYPES.pope`: scale 1.2, mass 2.2) and floating upright with open arms instead of flying like a missile. **Hits:** while the last regular wave runs, `G.lastHits` adds what every arrival takes to kill in shield units (`HIT_COST`: grunt 1, soldier 3, brute 6, jaw `JAW_HP`); the Pope gets twice that (`POPE_CFG.min` 20 at least), or `POPE_CFG.solo` 100 when started alone (`__obSim(60, 6, true)` measures the last wave of a default game at 47–59). `popeHit()`: each hit takes `DRAIN[kind]` (punch 1 … hammer 4), with blood and flinch but no shield or stun; every `POPE_CFG.combo` 10 hits, or any super move, charged punch, blast or bump, `shove`s him (`POPE_CFG.shove` 9 / mass, about 2.3 m) and he floats back in (≈ 3.7 s). At 0 he shatters (`G.popeDown`), and 4 s later the end screen says PAPIEŻ POKONANY. The HUD shows his hits left. Reaching him counts as a finished workout; the history records `mode` and `pope` (beaten).
- Kills trigger brief bullet-time (`ts = 0.18` for 0.35 s, then easing back).

### Moves

| Move | Input | Rule | Damage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cios (punch) | grip = fist | fist enters torso/head zone moving *away from the player* (velocity·away > 0.25) | 3 HP / 1 shield + shove |
| Dłoń bojowa (blade) | trigger only = blade hand | palm point through torso, head (`blade`) or neck (`neck`); 1 mana each | 4 HP, neck 6 / 2 shield; neck on a stunned enemy = decap fatality |
| Podbródkowy (uppercut) | grip = fist | fist rising into the chin (within 0.15 m of head − 7 cm) at `vel.y` > 1.4 m/s and > 60% of its speed; checked before the punch | 8 HP / 3 shield + shove with lift; kill `upper` (head launched up); fatality on a stunned brute |
| Młot (hammer) | both fists | fists < 0.25 m apart, both `vel.y` < −1.5 m/s, midpoint on the head or torso; both hands' cooldown 0.6 s | 10 HP / 4 shield + 1.5× shove; kill `smash` (shatter driven down); fatality on a stunned brute |
| Rozerwanie (rip) | both fists on a **stunned** torso | grab while both hands < 1.2 m/s, then pull them 0.3 m further apart **vertically** than at grab | fatality, torso splits at the waist |

- No upper speed limit. A hand slower than `MIN_SPEED` (0.35 m/s) doesn't register.
- **Knockback:** a non-lethal hit adds `(0.5 + min(speed,4)·0.35) / mass` m/s to the enemy's `kv` along the strike (damped, then sprung back to its spot), triggers a flinch (lean back, head snap, arms flung, twist), cuts 1.2 s off its windup and interrupts a swing.
- Per-hand cooldown per enemy: 0.5 s.
- **Drag and throw:** `u.grabEv` (true for 0.25 s after a hand closes into a fist, until used) with the hand slower than 1.4 m/s, within 0.18 m of an enemy fist or elbow, or near the head/torso, puts it in state `held` (`held()`): it lerps after the hand at `18/mass` per s. Releasing the grip faster than 1 m/s throws it (`shove` at `clamp(speed·3, 5, 12)/mass`, `e.thrown`); slower just drops it. After 2.5 s it breaks free into a swing. Not on stunned enemies (their grab is the rip). Thrown or shoved enemies can't pass behind the hull.
- **Stances** (`u.stance`): grip (`buttons[1]`) = `fist`, trigger (`buttons[0]`) = `blade`, nothing = `loose`; the fist wins when both are held. `u.grabEv` is the grab window after a hand closes (`u.grabT`, `u.grabUsed`).
- **Hands:** the hand models live in WebXR **grip space** (`getControllerGrip`): origin in the curled fingers, -Z along the handle towards the thumb, +X out of the back of the right hand, +Y up the forearm. `makeHand` builds the right hand (palm, 4 two-segment fingers, thumb, an 8 cm wrist-weight cuff tilted `CUFF_TILT` = 0.6 rad from +Y towards +Z, which is where the real forearm runs) and mirrors it with `scale.x = -1` for the left. **No forearm**: a straight +Y forearm made the elbows look too high. `poseHand(m, curl, blade)`. The model is rebuilt on `connected` once handedness is known. Fingers curl with `u.curl` (analog grip/trigger value, full fist when pressed). Gloves turn red with `u.blood` and drip.
- **Gore:** blood drops with gravity, chunks, meat, bone, guts and expanding blood mist (`blood`/`meat`/`guts`/`mist`). `bleed(obj, local, dir, rate, dur)` squirts from a point on a moving object (wounds, neck fountain, arm sockets, rip halves). `stump()` adds a raw cap and a bone stub. Wound splats stick to the chest (`wound`). Punch kills can tear off arms and the head. Particles are capped at 900.

### Enemies (`TYPES`)

- grunt / Szeregowy: no shield, one hit; olive robot
- soldier / Żołnierz: shield 2, then one hit; black and gold robot, blue field
- brute / Brutal: shield 4, then stun and fatality; scale 1.28, rust robot, red field
- The mix gets harder with wave number (`pickType`). The number alive at once follows from the strike ring: `strikeCap` attackers plus whatever of the 2 out-of-reach spots the bombers leave (`meleeFits`), so about 2 melee in wave 1 and 3–4 later.
- Models: `makeEnemy` builds a procedural low-poly rig: `g` (faces the player, +Z towards them) → `body` (lean) → `upper`/`lower` (split on rip), a `head` group (flies off on decap), `arms[]`/`legs[]`/`boots[]`, and `setCharge(f, showOrb)` / `dim()` helpers. When `robot.glb` has loaded (`window.__obReady`, which also waits for `pope.glb`), `fitRobot(e, M)` (M = `ROBOT` or `POPE`, both filled by `prepRobot(gltf, M)`; kept as `e.model`) hides the procedural meshes, moves the joints to the robot's proportions and adds a `SkeletonUtils.clone` of the robot with per-tier tints (`ROBOT_TINT`; 'Does not work' is the eyes+wings material, used as the visor/charge glow). `driveRobot` sets bone rotations every frame from the procedural joints' world rotations (`RIG`, rest offsets in `ROBOT.rest`). Fingers are curled into fists once (`ROBOT.curl`). `fistPos(e, i)` gives the robot's knuckles. Gore: `tear(e, part, group)` makes a static copy of a body piece (`ROBOT.parts`, triangles by dominant bone), posed with `boneWorld · boneInverse · bindMatrix`, hangs it on the procedural group that flies off, and collapses the bone on the live mesh. Pieces need `skinning:false` material copies (r128). Without the model (load failure, `file://`), the procedural enemies are used.
- Animation: `animate(e, gdt)` picks pose targets per state (approach/hover/windup/swing/grabbed/held/shoved/stunned), smooths them into `e.pose` (relative to `GUARD`) and layers `e.flinch` on top.

Inner-ring height is `anchor.y - 0.42 .. -0.20` (`innerDy`). Spawn cadence is `1.6 + rand*1.2 - min(round,6)*0.12` s; the first spawn of a wave comes 0.3 s in. With no melee enemy in play (wave start, or all killed) the next one appears 4.5–5.5 m out instead of 8–11 m.

## Structure of the script

settings + menu UI → three.js scene (stars, planet, platform, lights, head-following point light) → HUD canvas-texture planes (`hud`, `big`, `toast`, wrist `watches`) → hands in grip space (`hands[0..1].userData`: pos, palm, vel, speed, stance, grabEv, curl, bladeK, charged, blood, model) → WebAudio SFX (`bus` with compressor + limiter; recorded hits from `sfx/` layered with synth, see *Sound*) → enemies + `animate` → rings, spawn, `promote` → gore (particles, `bleed`) → `hit`/`shove`/`stun`/`finish`/`kill`, enemy swing (`startSwing`, `block`, `dodged`, `hitPlayer`, `knockout`) → grenades (`throwBomb`, `updateBombs`, `explode`) → upgrades and rest cards → `updateHands` → `thunderclap` → `combat(e)` → `approach`/`hold`/`shoved`/`separate` → `updateEnemies` → `loop()` → session start/end → results + history.

- `anchor` = head position/facing captured at the start and at each wave. Enemies and the HUD are placed relative to it, so the player can turn between waves.
- Reference space: `local-floor`.

## Storage (localStorage, per device)

`ob-cfg` (settings), `ob-hist` (last 20 sessions), `ob-tab` (last menu tab). All access is wrapped in try/catch. History records made before Oct 2026 have `control` and no `blows`/`blocks`/`dodges`, and the UI shows `–` for the missing fields.

## Testing without the headset

- **Dev mode (mouse)**: the *Tryb dev: graj myszką* button under *Wejdź do VR* (Trening tab) shows wherever VR isn't available, or with `?dev` in the address. It runs the real game on a flat canvas (`startDev`/`stopDev`, `devFrame` called first in `loop`; `updateHead` reads the plain camera when `dev` is set). Left/right button = left/right fist: `devAim` jabs it from the guard (`DEV_GUARD`) to the enemy, jaw, grenade, card or DALEJ nearest the cursor ray within `DEV_REACH` 0.8 m (else 0.6 m ahead); held, it stays out and follows the cursor (blocks). Space ducks 35 cm (fast enough to dodge a swing if pressed as it starts), A/D lean 30 cm, P pauses, Esc quits; the camera tilts further down in a break so the cards and DALEJ are in view. The hands get fake gamepads (`devPad`: `buttons[1]` = grip, `buttons[4]` = pause on the left pad only), so stances, grabs and pause run through `updateHands` unchanged. Blade hand, uppercut, hammer, rip, laser and god of speed aren't mapped. Dev fights aren't saved to the history. Headless browsers throttle `requestAnimationFrame` when the window isn't in front (Playwright: `page.bringToFront()`).

- `window.__obPreview(mode, w, h)` renders a static scene to a flat canvas with no XR needed. Modes: `telegraph` (one attacker straight ahead at a point of its attack: `window.__obPhase` 0..1 = windup start..impact, `window.__obSide` = three-quarter view, `window.__obType`; call `__obDebug().clearEnemies()` between frames, the preview never clears), `gameplay`, `ship` (looking back at the hull and doorway from the enemies' side), `swing`, `punch`, `decap`, `uppercut`, `hammer`, `rip`, `jaw`, `jawsplit`, `laser`, `pause`, `wrecks`, `stun`, `bombs`, `cards` (rest with upgrade cards and DALEJ), `hands` (close-up: left fist with the HP watch, right blade hand), `rest`, `done`, `roster`. Use it for screenshots (Playwright) or to eyeball models. Call it from the devtools console.
- `window.__obSim(sec, round, puncher)` runs the wave logic headlessly (still head, optional auto-jabbing right fist) and returns hits, KOs, kills, grenades thrown, `minGap` (the smallest torso clearance seen; it should stay ≥ 0) and `maxFar`/`avgFar` (enemies hovering out of reach, bombers included; blips of 3 during a turnaround are normal).
- `window.__obDebug()` returns the internals (`G`/`setG`, `enemies`, `bombs`, `hands`, `hit`, `throwBomb`, `updateEnemies`, `updateBombs`, `thunderclap`, `wantSpawn`, `freeAngle`, …) for scripted single-mechanic tests: place an enemy, fake a hand (`stance`, `pos`, `palm`, `vel`, `speed`, `grabEv`), step the updates and check the state.
- Playwright with swiftshader works for both: launch chromium with `--use-gl=angle --use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader`. Serve the repo over http (`python3 -m http.server` from the repo root, open `/bravr/index.html`) and `await window.__obReady` first. Over `file://` the robot can't load and you get the procedural enemies.
- For interactive testing on a PC, use **Immersive Web Emulator** (Meta's Chrome/Edge extension). It emulates a Quest with draggable controllers and buttons. Serve the folder locally (`npx serve bravr` or `python -m http.server`), because `localhost` counts as a secure context. Emulated motion is coarse, so `MIN_SPEED` and the punch-direction check can misfire.
- On the real headset, enable developer mode, connect USB, open `chrome://inspect` on the PC → inspect the Quest Browser tab for the console and live edits.

## Known gaps / next ideas

- Not yet tested on a real Quest 2. Hit radii, ring distances, block/dodge thresholds, the watch placement, `CUFF_TILT`, the grenade catch radius and the thunderclap detection probably need tuning after the first play.
- Balance is a guess: with no defence a still player takes about 20 hits a minute (grenades included); the auto-jabbing sim kills about 8 a minute.
- Robot gore pieces are posed in the model's bind pose (A-pose), not the current pose. Enemy hit zones are still the procedural ones, which roughly match the robot.
- Enemy punch aim is analytic (`aimArm` points the straight arm from the shoulder at your face); the robot's proportions differ a little from the procedural rig, so the knuckles can end a few cm off.
- No hand-tracking support (controllers only).
- HUD canvas text uses Chakra Petch only if the font has loaded before first draw. Otherwise it falls back to Arial Narrow.
- More ideas and the phase plan are in `BACKLOG.md`.
