# The Pope's voice lines (secret level)

Clips made with Voicemod. At each event below the game plays one clip, picked at random from that event's list
(`SAMPLES.popeStart`, `popeHit`, `popeHurt` in `../index.html`; the chances in `POPE_ODDS`). Clips in this folder
that aren't listed here are not used. Open this file's preview to listen.

## Start of the secret level

Plays as soon as the level begins (with the countdown when it's played on its own); the Pope comes down a couple of
seconds later.
