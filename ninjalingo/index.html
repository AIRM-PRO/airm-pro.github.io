Your Spanish: Food

How to play

  • You hold a saber in each hand. Swing through an object to cut it. Any direction counts, but the swing has to be fast.
  • Wrong cuts and misses drain the health bar, and more for words you already knew. At zero the session ends, but what you learned is kept.
  • In VR, pull a trigger to restart at the end, and press B or Y to leave. In the browser, move the mouse to swing.
